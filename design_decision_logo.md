# Decision Log: Application Logo (Eyes-on-Glass)

## What was decided
We decided to adopt the **"Option A - High-Tech & Cyber"** aesthetic for the primary macOS application icon. The design features a glowing, high-tech neon cybernetic eye/lens with intricate circuitry patterns in cyan and purple on a dark, rounded rectangle base.

## Alternatives considered
We explored four other distinct directions:
- **Option B:** Minimalist & Modern (Abstract geometric eye with film reel)
- **Option C:** Professional & Broadcast (Sleek broadcast monitor with SMPTE bars)
- **Option D:** Abstract & Fluid (Soft, vibrant UI gradients mimicking glass)
- **Option E:** The Wildcard (A literal physical lens over a glowing AI core)

## Why this option was chosen
The **"High-Tech & Cyber"** theme directly highlights the highly advanced, underlying MLX/Gemma-4 artificial intelligence nature of the platform. It communicates complex signal processing and visual data extraction, fitting perfectly with the "Eyes-on-Glass" nomenclature while pairing beautifully with the desktop app's premium dark-mode presentation.
